# Privacy Policy

**App:** Mozaik Atölyesi
**Developer:** Yunus Şahin
**Contact:** yunusahiin2005@gmail.com
**Last updated:** November 1, 2026

## Summary

- Mozaik Atölyesi is **fully playable offline**. You don't create an account, sign in,
  or provide any personal information.
- Your level progress, settings (theme, sound, haptics) and unfinished boards are stored
  **only on your device**. We run no servers, and this data is never sent anywhere.
- The app **contains ads** (Google AdMob). To serve ads, Google processes your device's
  advertising identifier and some technical information.
- The app contains **no** analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools.

## Advertising

Mozaik Atölyesi shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob:

- **Rewarded ads:** played only when you tap the "Hint" button; in return, one region of
  the board is solved for you. Watching is entirely optional.
- **Interstitial ads:** after the first 10 levels, a full-screen ad may appear between
  levels, on average once every 3 levels.

The whole game can be played without watching any ad.

To serve ads, Google processes your device's **advertising identifier** (Advertising ID
on Android; IDFA on iOS if you allow it) and technical information sent with the ad
request (such as device model, OS version, language, IP address for approximate
location, and ad interactions). This data is **shared with Google** and governed by
Google's policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not access or store this information.

### European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are in these regions, Google's **consent form** is shown the first time you open
the app and asks for your permission for personalised ads. You can decline; declining
does not stop you from playing. To change your choice later, use the
**"Gizlilik seçenekleri"** (Privacy options) link on the home screen.

### iOS: App Tracking Transparency

On iPhone and iPad, Apple's tracking permission is requested before the advertising
identifier (IDFA) can be used. If you don't allow it, ads are shown without
personalisation. You can change this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security →
Tracking**.

### Android: Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in **Settings → Privacy → Ads**.

## Children

Mozaik Atölyesi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal data from them.

## Data deletion

Progress and settings stored on your device are removed completely when you uninstall
the app. We hold no data about you. For Google's advertising data, see the Google links
above.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the
"Last updated" date above will change.
